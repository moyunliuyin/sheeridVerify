1. Accreditation & Institutional Overview

Founded in 1804, Ohio University is a comprehensive public research university accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), 230 South LaSalle Street, Suite 7-500, Chicago, IL 60604 (hlcommission.org). Ohio University operates on a semester calendar (Fall, Spring, and Summer semesters) with academic credit measured in semester hours.

2. Course Numbering System

1000–4999: Undergraduate lower- and upper-division courses.
5000–5999: Graduate courses (may be dual-listed with 4000-level undergraduate courses; graduate students complete additional advanced requirements).
6000–8999: Graduate-only courses, master's seminars, research, thesis, and doctoral dissertation courses.

4. Graduate Academic Standing & Degree Conferral

Graduate students at Ohio University must maintain a minimum cumulative grade-point average of 3.00 on all graduate coursework to remain in Good Academic Standing and qualify for graduation. Official degrees are posted only upon final certification by the Graduate College and the Office of the University Registrar.
